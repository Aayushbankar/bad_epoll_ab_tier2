Primitive Analysis

4 Dead-End Chains

Even when forcing synthetic race wins, every escalation path died.

  • ✕
    Refcount Corruptions: percpu_counter_dec operated on the valid outer object, not the freed inner struct.
  • ✕
    Dual-Watch KASLR Leak: Structurally impossible under checks in eventpoll.c:826.
  • ✕
    Fixed NULL Write: Decrement locked onto root_user, leaving only a fixed NULL write at offset 160.
  • ✕
    Struct Audit: Reachable kmalloc-192 objects (fib6_info, snd_timer_user) panic on NULL write.
