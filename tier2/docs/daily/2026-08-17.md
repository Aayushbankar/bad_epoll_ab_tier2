# Daily Log: 2026-08-17

## 🔍 Overview & Key Focus

Today conducted a comprehensive, ground-zero architectural reassessment of the **CVE-2026-46242 ("Bad Epoll")** vulnerability research. The evaluation systematically re-examined all prior assumptions by separating **software emulation artifacts (QEMU TCG / GDB)** from **physical ARM64 silicon behavior (asymmetric cores, weakly ordered memory barriers, cache coherency invalidation latency)**.

---

## 📋 Key Workstreams & Accomplishments

### 1. Physical Silicon vs. Emulation First-Principles Analysis
* Re-analyzed race schedulability from pure hardware physics:
  * On real heterogeneous ARM64 hardware (big.LITTLE / DynamIQ: Cortex-X4 vs Cortex-A520), asymmetric clock frequencies and cross-cluster interconnect delays (~20–80 ns) allow the race condition to trigger naturally without debugger intervention.
  * Disproved the assumption that natural race failure in QEMU TCG implies impossibility on bare-metal hardware.
* Re-evaluated memory corruption mechanics at **slab offset 160**:
  * Verified that on ARM64 GKI 6.1 (`kmalloc-192`), writing 8 bytes of NULL at offset 160 lands strictly inside `msg_msg` payload space (byte index 112) or invalidates non-controllable pointers.
  * Confirmed that even with 100% natural race success, the primitive remains structurally bounded to **Denial of Service (Kernel Panic / Reboot)** due to PAC, BTI, and forward-edge kCFI.
* **Analysis Dossier**: [Physical ARM64 Silicon vs Emulation First-Principles Analysis](docs/29_PHYSICAL_ARM64_SILICON_REBOOT_ANALYSIS.md)

### 2. Strategic Research Synthesis & Path Alignment
* **Path A (Physical Hardware Timebox)**: Confirmed that testing on physical ARM64 hardware is the scientifically required methodology to establish empirical ground truth on race schedulability.
* **Path C (Negative-Result Case Study)**: Formalized the research framing as an empirical study on why GKI 6.1 mitigations reduce a critical kernel UAF to DoS-only.
* **Path M (Vendor GPU Driver Primitives)**: Re-affirmed that for a functional untrusted-app to root shell exploit chain on Android, GPU driver vulnerabilities with deterministic page-level UAF and arbitrary physical memory R/W provide the proven path.
